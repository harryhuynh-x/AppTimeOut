Focus timer and schedule lock: block apps when it matters

Two simple ways to protect your attention: a focus timer for the next hour, and a schedule for the hours you always lose.

Timer Lock: for right now

Pick a duration, tap start, and your blocked apps and websites stay unavailable until the timer ends. Good for a study session, a deep-work block or a dinner without your phone. On the free plan, timers can run up to 8 hours.

Schedule Lock: set it once

Choose the days of the week and one or more time slots, for example 9:00–12:00 and 14:00–17:00 on weekdays. AppTimeOut applies the block when each slot starts and lifts it when it ends, every week, without you opening the app. The free plan includes up to 5 days and 4 time slots; Pro gives you all 7 days and unlimited slots.

Pick what to block

Add any apps and websites to your block list. On iPhone, blocking uses Apple's Screen Time system plus a Safari extension for websites. On Android, AppTimeOut uses the Accessibility Service to show a block screen when you open something on your list.

Make it harder to cheat

If you tend to switch blockers off, add a buddy. With Buddy Lock on, only a code from your buddy can end a lock early.

Available on iPhone and Android

AppTimeOut is free to start on both platforms, and your settings sync when you sign in.
