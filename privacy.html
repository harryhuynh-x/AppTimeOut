Privacy Policy

AppTimeOut — Simple, honest privacy.

Last updated: July 22, 2026

AppTimeOut ("we", "us", or "our") is an app developed and operated by AppTimeOut. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

  • Account info: Your email address and display name when you sign in with Apple, Google, or Email.
  • Blocked apps & websites: Names and identifiers of apps and websites you choose to block. These are stored to sync your block list across sign-ins.
  • Accountability buddies: Names and email addresses of buddies you add, used only to deliver unlock codes.
  • Usage data: Lock session timestamps and basic app usage statistics used to display your activity history.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect browsing history or content from blocked websites.
  • We do not collect Screen Time data beyond what you explicitly add to your block list.
  • Unlock codes are generated in-memory and are never stored on our servers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide app blocking and accountability features.
  • To send unlock code emails to your accountability buddies when you request one.
  • To sync your block list and buddies across sign-ins.

How Blocking Works on Your Device

To enforce the blocks you set up, AppTimeOut needs to detect when you open an app or website you have chosen to block. It does this using the blocking capabilities your operating system provides, and the details differ by platform:

  • On iOS, AppTimeOut uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. The system, not AppTimeOut, applies the shield to blocked apps and websites; AppTimeOut never sees your general app or web activity — only the apps and website categories you explicitly add to your block list.
  • On Android, AppTimeOut uses the Android Accessibility Service. The service reads the name of the app currently in the foreground and, when a supported web browser is open, the address (URL) in the browser's address bar, solely to detect when you open something you have chosen to block and show the block screen.

On both platforms, this information:

  • is processed entirely on your device, in real time;
  • is never collected, stored, transmitted off the device, sold, or shared with us or any third party;
  • is used only to enforce the blocks you configure — never for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

You can turn off the underlying permission at any time in your device's Settings (Screen Time access on iOS, the Accessibility Service on Android), which disables app and website blocking.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in the cloud and protected so that only you can access your own data.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen inside the app. Alternatively, contact us at support@apptimeout.com and we will remove your data within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

AppTimeOut is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
